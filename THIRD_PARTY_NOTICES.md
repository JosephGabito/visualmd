# Third-party notices

Visual MD depends on open-source packages whose licences are available through
Flutter's licence page in the app. Bundled typeface notices live beside their
font files under `assets/fonts/`.

The Shiki worker carries TextMate grammars, themes, and supporting libraries
with additional notices. The exact notice shipped by the pinned
`shiki_flutter` package is preserved at
[`assets/licenses/shiki_flutter-1.1.0-THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](assets/licenses/shiki_flutter-1.1.0-THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
and appears in the app's licence page.

## Adapted colour palettes

Visual MD includes reader-specific adaptations of these published colour
systems. Their original projects and copyright notices are:

- [Catppuccin](https://github.com/catppuccin/palette), copyright 2021
  Catppuccin, MIT License.
- [Everforest](https://github.com/sainnhe/everforest), copyright 2019 sainnhe,
  MIT License.
- [Gruvbox](https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox), created by Pavel Pertsev and
  distributed under the MIT/X11 License.
- [Nord](https://www.nordtheme.com), copyright 2016-present Sven Greb, MIT
  License.
- [Rosé Pine](https://github.com/rose-pine/rose-pine-theme), copyright 2023
  Rosé Pine, MIT License.
- [Solarized](https://ethanschoonover.com/solarized/), copyright 2011 Ethan
  Schoonover, MIT License.

Visual MD's semantic mappings and contrast adjustments are described in
